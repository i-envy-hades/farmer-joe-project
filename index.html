Farmer Joe Publisher

Farmer Joe Publisher is a private, internal tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel Farmer Joe's Farm - Stories, an original animated series for preschoolers.

It uploads the channel's own finished episodes to that single channel on a fixed schedule, using the YouTube Data API. Each upload is marked made for kids and starts as private; a person reviews every episode before it is uploaded and before it is made public.

What it is not

It is not a public app. Nobody else can sign in to it, it has no users besides the channel owner, and it does not read, collect or store any information about viewers or other YouTube users.
