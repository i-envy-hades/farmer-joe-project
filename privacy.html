Privacy Policy

Farmer Joe Publisher · Last updated 18 September 2026

Farmer Joe Publisher ("the tool") is an internal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Farmer Joe's Farm - Stories. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What information the tool accesses

The only account the tool connects to is the channel owner's own Google account. With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), it receives an access token that lets it:

  • upload the channel's own videos and set their thumbnails;
  • read and change the privacy status of those same videos (for example, private to public).

It does not access comments, subscribers, viewers, analytics, email, contacts or any other Google data, and it never accesses any other person's account.

How that information is used and stored

The access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner, and is used only to perform the actions listed above. Records of which episodes were uploaded (title, upload date, YouTube video id) are kept in a local database on the same computer.

Sharing

The tool does not share, sell or transfer any information to anyone. It uses no cookies, analytics or advertising, and this website does not track visitors.

Children

The channel's videos are made for children and are declared as such to YouTube. The tool itself collects no information from children or from any viewer.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes all stored access.
